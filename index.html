Jane

A private voice assistant that runs on one computer in one home. It is not a product, is not offered to the public, and has one user: its owner.

What Jane is

Jane answers spoken questions and controls devices in her owner's home, such as lights, a television and a thermostat. She runs on a computer in that home. Nobody else can sign in to her or use her.

Privacy notice

This notice covers Jane's use of Google services. It applies to the Google accounts of her owner's household, which are the only accounts that ever sign in.

What Jane accesses.

  • Nest thermostat information through Google's Smart Device Management API: the thermostats in the home, their temperature, humidity, mode and set points. Jane reads these and changes set points and modes when her owner asks.
  • Google Sheets and Google Drive files that Jane herself creates, such as a household inventory and a shopping list. She cannot see other files in the account.
  • If her owner turns it on, sending email from Jane's own Google account.

Where it is kept. The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored in the system keychain of the home computer Jane runs on. Jane keeps a log of the questions she is asked and what she did, in a file on that same computer. Nothing is stored on any server run for Jane.

Who it is shared with. Nobody. Data from Google is not sold, not used for advertising, and not given to any other person or company, with one exception that is part of how Jane works: to answer a question, the text of the question and the information needed to answer it (for example, the current thermostat reading or the items on the shopping list) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes her reply, and the reply's text is sent to ElevenLabs, which turns it into speech. Those services process it to return the answer.

How long it is kept. Until her owner deletes it. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes the stored tokens useless.

Google's rules. Jane's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact. Jane's owner, at the support email shown on her Google sign-in screen.
